# Scroll Harbor hero: script (approved by Adam)

How it plays: as the visitor scrolls, the video moves through the story and one short line fades in at a time (about 3 lines per scene, each line fully readable before the next arrives). Lines are real HTML text over the video, not baked into it. Every line is either an empathy line (no factual claim) or a fact Adam confirmed.

| Scene (still) | Mood | Lines, in order | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Clouds gather | Calm before | "It doesn't start as a storm." | Empathy |
| 2. The storm | Overwhelm | "The worry that won't stop." / "The arguments every night." / "Coping the only way you know how." | Empathy |
| 3. Lost | Stuck | "Calls that go unanswered." / "Weeks on a waitlist." / "Forms that go nowhere." | Empathy, from what people say about getting care |
| 4. The light | Turning point | "Then, one call." / "In within 48 hours of finished paperwork." / "Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, United Healthcare." | Facts (Adam confirmed) |
| 5. The harbor | Calm returns | "The same counselor, every visit." / "Children, teens, and adults." / "Recovery support, without judgment." | Facts (Adam confirmed; "without judgment" approved by Adam) |
| 6. Peace | Relief | (no new line, the family steps onto the dock) | |
| 7. Home | Joy | **"Every storm has a safe harbor."** (the tagline, the page's H1) then simply: **"Give us a call."** / button: **(918) 553-5746** / small: "Safe Harbor Behavioral Health, Tulsa" | Tagline + one plain invitation (kept simple on purpose, not salesy) |

Notes
- The tagline is the page's one H1. It is in the page text from the first load (Google reads it, not hidden with display:none), and the on-screen reveal happens at the end of the scroll. Adam chose: reveal only at the end.
- Decided by Adam: scene 2's third line is softened to "Coping the only way you know how."; "Recovery support, without judgment" is approved.
- "Weeks on a waitlist" and "Calls that go unanswered" are general experiences, not claims about any named provider. No competitor is named.
- The 988 crisis banner stays visible above everything.
- No "therapy" or "therapist" in the lines (allowed, but not needed).
- Phones: same lines over the stills until a portrait video exists.
- Keyword text ("Counseling and recovery support in Tulsa for children, teens, and adults") moves out of the overlay into the normal page text just below the hero, so the end of the film stays simple and Google still gets the words.
